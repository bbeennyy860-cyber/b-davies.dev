### Visual Metaphor: The Quant Command Center
A living data visualization - like looking at a quant workstation where data streams, signals flow, and systems pulse with life.

### Color Palette
- Background: Deep space gradient #08080c to #0c0a1a to #0a0f1a (NOT flat black)
- Primary accent: Electric violet #8b5cf6
- Secondary accent: Warm amber #f59e0b  
- Text primary: #e2e8f0, muted: #94a3b8, dim: #475569
- Card glass: rgba(15,15,25,0.7) with backdrop-filter blur(20px)
- Borders: rgba(139,92,246,0.15)
- Glow on hover: box-shadow 0 0 30px rgba(139,92,246,0.3)
